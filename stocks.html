S&P 1500 members: multiples vs their own history

Every current S&P 1500 member, ranked by market cap, from Bloomberg G 52 (individual fundamental). Each multiple is the share price (or enterprise value) over Bloomberg's blended-forward consensus (BEst) figure, every trading day since 2002. Z-score = (current − mean) ÷ standard deviation over the chosen history; percentile = share of days in that history at or below today's value. Days with a zero or negative denominator (losses) are left out; stats need at least ~6 months of values. Red = rich vs its own past, teal = cheap. Click a row for its history.
