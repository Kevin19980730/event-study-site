Quick:
Month return = last close of the month ÷ last close of the month before − 1; year return is the YTD convention at year end. A series' first partial month is not counted, and the month and year still in progress are shown (dashed) but kept out of every statistic. Monthly returns don't overlap, so t — each month's average against the all-month average, in standard errors — is a fair test here; |t| ≥ 2 is highlighted.
